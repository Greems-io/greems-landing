For employees

Book it, charge it, done.

Charging is free — just share fairly. Reserve a charger for a half-day, activate it when you arrive, and free the bay when you’re done so the next person can charge.

1

Sign in

Open the app on your phone — nothing to install.
  1. Scan the QR at the charger to open the Charging Pool portal.
  2. Enter your work email (@corporate.com) — or tap Sign in with Microsoft if your company uses it.
  3. You're in — no password, no waiting. instant
Your manager approves who can charge. If email verification is switched on, you'll get a one-tap sign-in link instead.
2

Book a charger

Reserve a charger for a half-day slot, so it’s waiting for you — no circling the car park.
  1. In the Booking tab, pick a day and a slot (e.g. Morning or Afternoon).
  2. Choose a free charger and tap Book.
  3. It shows up under My booking. reserved
Each charger holds one booking per slot, and you get a set number of bookings per week (your manager decides how many, how far ahead, and which days).
💡 Tip: Book the day before for a busy morning — you’ll have a guaranteed bay when you arrive.
3

Find a charger

Every charger, its status, its plug and its speed — at a glance.
Available In use Out of order
Type 2AC · everyday top-up
CCS2DC · the fast one
Match the icon to your car, and each bay shows its max speed (e.g. up to 22 kW). In-use chargers show a “free by ~HH:MM” estimate so you know when one opens up.
4

Start charging

Activate your booked charger the moment your slot begins.
  1. When your slot is live, open My booking and tap Activate charging.
  2. Only you can start the charger you booked, during your slot — no one can jump your bay.
  3. The card flips to your active session.
5

While you charge

Track your time and know exactly when to head back.
  • ⏱️Live timerWatch your elapsed charging time.
  • 🕒Free by HH:MMYour start time plus the pool’s max session length.
  • ⏹️Stop chargingEnd your session when you’re done. One session at a time.
  • 🔔Running late?If you don’t activate your booking, we’ll email to ask if you’re still coming — tap Cancel to free it for a colleague.
There are fewer chargers than drivers, so sessions have a time limit — when yours is up, please move your car so a colleague can charge.
💡 Tip: Watch for your “time’s almost up” reminder so you’re never the one blocking a bay.
